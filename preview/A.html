> OUR TOOLS

Small tools, in the present tense.

Drafting and Sliding are the -ing family — always being made. Coxpit runs the machines that make the rest. Click any tile above to open it.

Why self-hostable?

Because the useful part of a tool shouldn't depend on someone else's server, subscription, or uptime. AIP Lab tools keep your work in a local file and let the AI help without taking it away.

Bring your own key — or your Claude subscription. Run it from your terminal and it just works. No accounts, nothing to sign up for.

Made by one person

AIP Lab is built by hanmariyang — one maker, small tools, shipped in public.
