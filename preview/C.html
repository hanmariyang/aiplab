One maker, small tools

AIP Lab is built by hanmariyang — one person, shipping small self-hostable tools in public. Each has its own mascot, its own colour, its own repo.

Bring your own key — or your Claude subscription. Run any of them from your terminal and it just works. No server to trust, no account to make.

Why self-hostable?

Because the useful part of a tool shouldn't depend on someone else's server, subscription, or uptime. These keep your work in a local file and let the AI help without taking it away.

# any AIP Lab tool, one line
$ npx @hanmariyang/drafting serve
# → http://localhost:8477

# your data stays here
