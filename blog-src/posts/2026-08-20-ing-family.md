Drafting. Sliding. Lighting. Three of the tools share a shape in their names, and it isn't an accident. The `-ing` is the present continuous: something happening now, still in motion, not finished. That's how the studio thinks about software. A tool is never done. It's being made, in the present tense.

## One rule across all of them

Names aside, there's a rule every tool obeys. The AI proposes, you accept, and only then does anything become real. In Drafting a sentence is a suggestion until you accept it into the document. In Working a plan is a suggestion until you accept it into a file. Same rule, different surface.

> The AI writes the first draft. You write the document.

It keeps the human where it matters. Not approving a machine's output line by line as a chore, but deciding what is worth keeping.

## Small, self-hostable, yours

Coxpit doesn't end in -ing, and Working is a native app, not a web tool. What ties the family together isn't the suffix. It's that each one is small, runs on your own machine, keeps your data in a plain file, and is open source. The name is a wink. The rule is the point.
